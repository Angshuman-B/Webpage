Research

What I work on

I am building a small-scale testbed at IIT Bombay for evaluating connected and autonomous vehicles in mixed traffic. On it, I work on motion planning, multi-vehicle coordination, safety evaluation and simulation studies. I am also mentoring the team developing an autonomous campus shuttle at IIT Bombay.

Cyber-physical testbeds

Small-scale vehicles and simulation working together, for developing and validating connected and autonomous vehicles in mixed traffic.

Planning and coordination

Trajectory and path planning, and multi-agent reinforcement learning for unsignalised intersections.

Safety and Physical AI

Safety validation of autonomous systems, and hardware for autonomous vehicles and road sensing.
